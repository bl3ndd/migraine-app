# Migraine Log — Privacy Policy

The live copy is `site/privacy.html` (deployed by Vercel), whose URL is in `MigraineLog/Models/AppLinks.swift` and goes into App Store Connect (guideline 5.1.1(i)). Keep the two in sync.

_Last updated: October 31, 2026_

**Short version: Migraine Log never collects your health data. Your diary stays on your devices and in Apple Health. We never see it.**

## What the app stores
The headaches you log (start and end time, pain level, type, where it hurts, symptoms, possible triggers, medicines and whether they helped, and notes) and the list of medicines you save are stored only on your device. Migraine Log has no account, no servers of its own and no advertising. The only data that leaves your device is the anonymous usage statistics described below.

## Apple Health
If you turn on Apple Health in Settings, Migraine Log:
- **writes** the headaches you finish logging to Apple Health as Headache samples (mild, moderate or severe), with the details you entered attached;
- **reads** Headache samples from Apple Health, including ones logged by other apps.

Health data is used only to show your diary, insights and reports in the app. It is never shared with anyone, never used for advertising or marketing, and never stored in iCloud by Migraine Log. You can turn off access at any time in the iOS Settings app › Health › Data Access & Devices › Migraine Log.

## Anonymous usage statistics
Migraine Log sends anonymous usage signals through TelemetryDeck (telemetrydeck.com): the name of an action (for example "report created") with a simple setting such as the report period or whether Pro is unlocked, plus technical details (app version, device model, iOS version, language, region, time zone, display and accessibility settings, session counts) and an identifier hashed on your device. Signals **never** include your headaches, pain levels, symptoms, triggers, medicines, notes, Apple Health data or anything you type. Turn them off in Settings › Privacy › Share anonymous usage stats.

## Backups and reports
When you tap "Back Up Diary" or create a PDF report, the app creates a file and you choose where to save or send it. Migraine Log does not upload these files anywhere.

## Purchases
Purchases are handled by Apple. We don't receive your payment details.

## Deleting your data
Delete individual entries from the Calendar or the entry screen, or delete the app to remove all data stored by it. Headaches already saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app. We hold no copy, so there is nothing to request from us.

## Children
Migraine Log is not directed at children under 13.
