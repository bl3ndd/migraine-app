Common questions

Can I log a headache that's still going?

Yes. Tap Log Headache, pick the pain level and save with It's over switched off. Home then shows it as in progress; tap It's Over when it ends, or Update to add medicine or change the pain level.

What's the difference between headache days and migraine days?

A headache day is any day with a logged headache or migraine; a migraine day is a day with an entry marked Migraine. A day counts once however many attacks it had, and an attack that runs past midnight counts on both days. These are the numbers neurologists usually ask for.

I'm getting a new iPhone. How do I keep my diary?

  • Apple Health: with sync turned on, finished headaches are saved to Apple Health with their details. On the new iPhone, install Migraine Log, turn on Apple Health in Settings, and your diary comes back.
  • Backup file: Settings › Back Up Diary saves a file wherever you choose. On the new iPhone, use Restore from Backup.

How do I share my diary with my doctor?

Tap Doctor Report on the Today or Insights screen, choose a period (neurologists usually ask for the last 3 months), and tap Create PDF Report. You can then print it, email it, or save it. The report is part of Migraine Log Pro.

The reminder doesn't show up.

Check that notifications are allowed for Migraine Log in the iOS Settings app › Notifications, then set the time in Migraine Log › Settings › Reminder.

How do I delete an entry?

Open it and tap Delete Entry, or long-press it in the Calendar. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone. Headaches saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app. We don't hold a copy of your diary.

Important: Migraine Log is a diary, not a medical device, and doesn't give medical advice. Always talk to your doctor about your headaches. Call 911 or your local emergency number for the worst headache of your life, a sudden "thunderclap" headache, or a headache with weakness, numbness, confusion, trouble speaking, a stiff neck with fever, or after a head injury.
