Simply BP Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Simply BP never collects your health data. Your readings stay on your devices and in Apple Health. We never see them.

What the app stores

The blood pressure readings you enter (top and bottom number, pulse, date and time, arm, position, tags and notes) are stored only on your device. Simply BP has no account, no servers of its own and no advertising. The only data that leaves your device is the anonymous usage statistics described below.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health in the app's Settings, Simply BP:

  • writes the readings you enter to Apple Health as blood pressure and heart rate samples;
  • reads blood pressure and heart rate samples from Apple Health, including ones written by your blood pressure monitor's app.

Health data is used only to show your readings, statistics and reports in the app. It is never shared with anyone, never used for advertising or marketing, and never stored in iCloud by Simply BP. Syncing Apple Health between your devices is handled by Apple under your iCloud settings. You can turn off access at any time in the iOS Settings app › Health › Data Access & Devices › Simply BP.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which features people use, Simply BP sends anonymous usage signals through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. A signal contains the name of an action (for example "report created") with a simple setting such as the chart period or whether Pro is unlocked, plus technical details: app version, device model, iOS version, language, region and time zone, display and accessibility settings (such as text size), how many sessions and days the app was used, and an identifier that is hashed on your device, so it can't be traced back to you.

Signals never include your readings, categories, pulse, tags, notes, Apple Health data or anything you type. They are not used for advertising or tracking. You can turn them off at any time in the app: Settings › Privacy › Share anonymous usage stats.

Backups and reports

When you tap "Back Up Readings" or create a PDF report, the app creates a file and you choose where to save or send it. Simply BP does not upload these files anywhere.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We don't receive your payment details.

Deleting your data

Delete individual readings in History, or delete the app to remove all data stored by it. Readings already saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app. We hold no copy, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Simply BP is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
