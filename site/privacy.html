Migraine Log Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Migraine Log never collects your health data. Your diary stays on your devices and in Apple Health. We never see them.

What the app stores

The headaches you log (start and end time, pain level, type, where it hurts, symptoms, possible triggers, medicines and whether they helped, and notes) and the list of medicines you save are stored only on your device. Migraine Log has no account, no servers of its own and no advertising. The only data that leaves your device is the anonymous usage statistics described below.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health in the app's Settings, Migraine Log:

  • writes the headaches you finish logging to Apple Health as Headache samples (mild, moderate or severe), with the details you entered attached;
  • reads Headache samples from Apple Health, including ones logged by other apps.

Health data is used only to show your diary, insights and reports in the app. It is never shared with anyone, never used for advertising or marketing, and never stored in iCloud by Migraine Log. Syncing Apple Health between your devices is handled by Apple under your iCloud settings. You can turn off access at any time in the iOS Settings app › Health › Data Access & Devices › Migraine Log.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which features people use, Migraine Log sends anonymous usage signals through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. A signal contains the name of an action (for example "report created") with a simple setting such as the report period or whether Pro is unlocked, plus technical details: app version, device model, iOS version, language, region and time zone, display and accessibility settings (such as text size), how many sessions and days the app was used, and an identifier that is hashed on your device, so it can't be traced back to you.

Signals never include your headaches, pain levels, symptoms, triggers, medicines, notes, Apple Health data or anything you type. They are not used for advertising or tracking. You can turn them off at any time in the app: Settings › Privacy › Share anonymous usage stats.

Backups and reports

When you tap "Back Up Diary" or create a PDF report, the app creates a file and you choose where to save or send it. Migraine Log does not upload these files anywhere.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We don't receive your payment details.

Deleting your data

Delete individual entries from the Calendar or the entry screen, or delete the app to remove all data stored by it. Headaches already saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app. We hold no copy, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Migraine Log is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
